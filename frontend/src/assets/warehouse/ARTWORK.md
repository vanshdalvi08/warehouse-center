# Warehouse login artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 18 November 2026. These project-local PNGs replace the original hand-coded character drawings. All three outputs were visually reviewed for anatomy, object separation, perspective and relevant warehouse actions. The slideshow retains its existing crossfade and controls; the artwork itself is static.

Files: `receiving.png`, `storage.png`, `dispatch.png`.

## Prompt set

Each prompt used these shared directions:

Use case: illustration-story. Create one polished professional editorial vector-style warehouse illustration for a minimal website login carousel. Landscape 3:2 composition. Crisp fine dark navy outlines, flat periwinkle blue #5476ef, pale blue #e6ecff, white, restrained shading. Solid very pale blue background #f4f6fd. Calm spacious composition with generous empty margins, all subjects fully within frame. Anatomically correct hands, coherent perspective and believable object scale. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Not a UI screenshot. Beautiful clean commercial illustration, understated and precise.

Receiving subject: A warehouse receiving employee with realistic natural human proportions, blue work vest and blue cap, holding a small handheld barcode scanner and scanning a clearly separate cardboard box on a waist-high receiving bench. Other hand rests naturally on the bench. Behind them a simple warehouse shelf with neatly placed cartons.

Storage subject: A warehouse employee with realistic natural human proportions wearing a periwinkle blue work vest and blue cap, placing ONE modest sized cardboard carton onto an accessible waist-high shelf with both hands naturally supporting the box. Show full figure and coherent warehouse shelving, with neatly stored cartons and enough space to work.

Dispatch subject: Dispatch packing station in a warehouse: one employee with realistic natural human proportions wearing a periwinkle blue work vest and blue cap, standing behind a waist-high packing bench, using a handheld tape dispenser to seal ONE cardboard shipping carton. A realistic full-size blue delivery van is parked behind and to the right at the loading bay, clearly spatially separate and further in the background, consistent perspective.
